Privacy Policy – TapSet Score

Last updated: November 2026

TapSet Score respects your privacy. This application is designed to help users keep score during tennis and padel matches.

1. Data Collection

No account creation is required to use TapSet Score. The application does not ask users to provide their name, email address or other personal contact information.

Match information, including scores and player names entered by users, is used for the application's scoring features. This information is stored locally on the device, unless otherwise indicated by a feature explicitly described in the app.

2. Analytics

TapSet Score uses Firebase Analytics, a service provided by Google, to understand general application usage and improve the application.

The analytics events are intended to measure application sessions and match creation. Match scores, individual points and match results are not intended to be collected through these analytics events.

Firebase Analytics may process technical information such as device and application information, usage information and technical identifiers. The exact information processed depends on the application's configuration and the settings of the analytics service.

Google may process this information on behalf of the application publisher in accordance with its applicable privacy terms.

3. Apple Watch

TapSet Score supports use with a compatible Apple Watch. Match scoring information can be synchronized between the paired iPhone and Apple Watch to provide the scoring features.

This synchronization is used to operate the application and is not intended to publish match information to other users.

4. Data Sharing

TapSet Score does not sell users' personal information. Analytics information may be processed by Google through Firebase Analytics as described in this policy.

5. Data Retention

Match information stored locally remains on the user's device until it is deleted by the user or the application is removed. Analytics data is retained according to the configuration of Firebase Analytics and the applicable service policies.

6. Children's Privacy

TapSet Score is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. No account or personal contact information is required to use the application.

7. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when application features or data processing practices change. The latest update date will be displayed at the top of this page.
