Privacy Policy

TapSet Score – Padel & Tennis

Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how TapSet Score handles information when you use the application.

2. Personal Information and User Accounts

TapSet Score does not require users to create an account and does not ask users to provide personal contact information to use the app.

3. Match Data Stored on Your Device

Match information, including player or team names and scores, is stored locally on your device to provide the app's features. This information is not intended to be collected through Firebase Analytics.

Match data may be synchronized between your paired iPhone and Apple Watch to support the companion app experience.

4. Analytics

TapSet Score uses Firebase Analytics, a service provided by Google, to understand basic application usage and improve the app.

The application is intended to measure only the following events:

  • Application openings and sessions.
  • Creation of a match.

The application is not intended to use Analytics to record individual points, match scores, match results, or match completion events.

Firebase Analytics may also process technical information associated with application usage, such as app version, device and operating system information, usage information and technical identifiers. The exact information processed depends on the application's configuration and the settings of the analytics service.

5. Third-Party Services

Analytics data is processed by Google through Firebase Analytics. Google may process this information on behalf of the application publisher in accordance with its applicable privacy terms.

For more information, please consult:

  • Google's Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security information

6. Apple Watch

TapSet Score supports use with a compatible Apple Watch. Match scoring information can be synchronized between the paired iPhone and Apple Watch to provide the scoring features.

This synchronization is used to operate the application and is not intended to publish match information to other users.

7. Data Sharing and Sale

TapSet Score does not sell users' personal information. Analytics information may be processed by Google through Firebase Analytics as described in this policy.

8. Data Retention

Match information stored locally remains on the user's device until it is deleted by the user or the application is removed, subject to device backup settings.

Analytics data is retained according to the Firebase Analytics configuration and Google's applicable service policies.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your country of residence, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data. For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact us using the email address below.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features or data processing practices change. The latest update date will be displayed at the top of this page.
