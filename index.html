Privacy Policy

TapSet Score – Padel & Tennis

Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how TapSet Score handles information when you use the application.

2. Personal Information and User Accounts

TapSet Score does not require users to create an account and does not ask users to provide personal contact information to use the app.

3. Match Data Stored on Your Device

Match information, including player or team names and scores, is stored locally on your device to provide the app's features. This information is not intentionally collected through Firebase Analytics.

Match data may be synchronized between your paired iPhone and Apple Watch to support the companion app experience.

4. Analytics

TapSet Score uses Firebase Analytics, a service provided by Google, to understand basic application usage and improve the app.

The app is intended to measure only the following events:

  • Application openings and sessions.
  • Creation of a match.

TapSet Score does not use analytics to record individual points, match scores, match results, or match completion events.

Firebase Analytics may also process certain technical information associated with app usage, such as app version, device and operating system information, and technical identifiers, depending on the Firebase configuration and the user's device settings.

5. Third-Party Services

Analytics data is processed by Google through Firebase Analytics. Google processes this information in accordance with its own privacy and data protection terms.

For more information, please consult Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security information .

6. Data Retention

Match data stored locally remains on your device until you delete it or remove the application, subject to your device's backup settings.

Analytics data is retained according to the Firebase Analytics configuration and Google's applicable retention policies.

7. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell users' personal information. Data may be processed by the service providers described in this policy to provide analytics services.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your country of residence, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data. For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact us using the email address below.
